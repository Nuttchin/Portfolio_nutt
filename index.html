2D/3D Game Development & Interactive Experiences Showcase

ROCKET DRIFT

ROCKET DRIFT is a 2D arcade survival space game developed with Unity and C#. Players navigate an open cosmic expanse to rescue lost rockets and form an ever-growing convoy while evading hazardous planets and tracking meteors.

As the journey progresses, dynamic swarms of cosmic obstacles and hostile bosses escalate in speed and frequency. Players must use agile touch-steering, thruster boosts, and clever tactical maneuvers to bait bosses into colliding with dense planets to deal damage.

Features a responsive touch/mouse drag control system, smooth follower queue mechanics, off-screen viewport spawners, and rewarded video revives via Unity Ads.

BABY MAYBE CRY

BABY MAYBE CRY is a PC horror, strategy, and survival game exploring childhood fears of nighttime monsters. A child awakes in the dead of night to find a mysterious dimensional door where the hallway should be, opening into an unfamiliar haunted mansion.

Players must navigate the eerie halls, locate the target room, scavenge valuable items with limited durability, and deliver them to unlock the escape portal while defending against Player-Hunter and Item-Destroyer demons.

As 3D Artist, Level Designer and Game Designer, I crafted atmospheric room environments, architectural floor plans (Map 1 & Map 2), hand-painted textures, and the strategic room-locking mechanic to protect collected resources.

Hi, I’m Nutt Chinsakonthanagorn!

I’m a Media Technology (Game Development) student who has been in love with games for as long as I can remember, both playing across all sorts of genres and designing them. My drive comes from a simple mindset: "give it a shot and see what happens." I love jumping into new challenges because every experiment adds a fresh skill and a broader perspective to my toolkit.

For me, making games is all about people. I genuinely love communication and believe the best ideas come from bouncing thoughts off each other, sharing mentorship, and making sure everyone across the team feels heard and aligned. There’s honestly nothing better than pitching a vision and seeing the whole crew bring it across the finish line together.

When diving into a project, I'm all about pushing creative boundaries while keeping players at heart. A huge part of my craft is 3D modeling—I love shaping assets from scratch and bringing ideas into tangible space. Whether it's crafting an immersive world or sweating the small details until every feature clicks, I genuinely enjoy the process of making things come alive.

At the end of the day, I get a real kick out of supporting the people around me. I strongly believe in passionate discourse, teamwork, and checking egos at the door to make the best games possible.
