ROCKET DRIFT

ROCKET DRIFT is a 2D arcade survival space game developed with Unity and C#. Players navigate an open cosmic expanse to rescue lost rockets and form an ever-growing convoy while evading hazardous planets and tracking meteors.

As the journey progresses, dynamic swarms of cosmic obstacles and hostile bosses escalate in speed and frequency. Players must use agile touch-steering, thruster boosts, and clever tactical maneuvers to bait bosses into colliding with dense planets to deal damage.

Features a responsive touch/mouse drag control system, smooth follower queue mechanics, off-screen viewport spawners, and rewarded video revives via Unity Ads.

BABY MAYBE CRY

BABY MAYBE CRY is a PC horror, strategy, and survival game exploring childhood fears of nighttime monsters. A child awakes in the dead of night to find a mysterious dimensional door where the hallway should be, opening into an unfamiliar haunted mansion.

Players must navigate the eerie halls, locate the target room, scavenge valuable items with limited durability, and deliver them to unlock the escape portal while defending against Player-Hunter and Item-Destroyer demons.

As 3D Artist and Level Designer, I crafted atmospheric room environments, architectural floor plans (Map 1 & Map 2), hand-painted textures, and the strategic room-locking mechanic to protect collected resources.
